01 · CAPTURE
Bank SMS can become expenses.
When you open Dime, we show new bank messages. You keep or skip. Nothing is logged behind your back.
02 · LOG IT YOUR WAY
Receipt, voice, or type.
Snap a bill, say “two hundred chai,” or enter it. Same ledger, three doors.
03 · BUDGETS
Limits with room left.
Monthly, daily, category. The app tells you when you are close — not after you are through.
04 · INSIGHTS
Who you paid. What it cost.
Cashflow, payees, compare this month to last. The indigo is for the number. Everything else stays quiet.
